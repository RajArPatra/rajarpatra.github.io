Week Load

How full each week of my term actually is, read straight off my calendar. One row per week, one cell per day, darker where the day is fuller. Hours and counts only — no titles, no places, no people.

[ week by week ]
Hours booked in a day none under 2 2–4 4–6 6 plus day with a double-booking today

What counts as a double-booking. Two different events overlapping by more than five minutes. The same event entered twice is merged rather than counted as a clash, and so are overlaps with anything marked Free.

How a day is counted. Overlapping events are merged for the hours, so a double-booked hour is still one hour. Only time between 7:00 and 23:00 counts, so an overnight or multi-day block reads as a full day rather than 24 hours of meetings. Public holiday and birthday calendars are left out.
